"*The tavern is quite small and simple as befits a humble farming village. As you enter you smell rosemary and pot roast with potatoes. There are four booths and one long table, and a bar to your right. The bar is tended by an intimidating orc whose aura is softened by a friendly smile and a rounded belly. A much younger orc is scrubbing something difficult out of the floor in a corner by the backdoor. There is a bulletin board on the wall near the door and above the bar is a sign that reads "Now Serving Wizard's Tower Brewing Company Pale Ale!!!" with three exclamation points*."
