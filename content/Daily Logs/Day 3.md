17 Kythorn, 1325 DR

The adventurers awaken at the Entwhistle campsite. (8 am)

- The party has a big naked bath party in the lake
- At [[The Quail's Egg]], the party expresses gratitude to [[Crognar Crumpknot]] and enjoys a lavish breakfast spread, orange juice included.
- Visiting a hungover [[Fildara]], the party is given a Sending Stone and asked to deliver it to [[Thrammel]] at [[The Careless Heart]] in [[The Outskirts]] of [[The City of Millspar]]. Care must be taken not to expose Thrammel as a Harper agent as [[The Thieves Guild]] holds sway over the Outskirts.
- Setting out on the road to Millspar, [[Rickward]] appears, begging the party to help him dissuade [[Dakota]] from joining with a band of scary cultists.
- The party, with Rickward leading the way, explores ancient ruins looking for a tower where they expect to ambushed by Dakota and the cultists. 
- Dakota and the cultists do appear and attack the party. Dakota seems very upset with Rickward for upsetting the plan. The Cult Leader is driven to retreat, but leaves a telepathic message: "You have made very powerful enemies today".
- Dakota runs off, and Rickward runs after her.
- The party manages to bludgeon the door to the tower until it crumbles and explores inside.
- There is a large metal plate covering a hole in the floor. Attempts to move it are unsuccessful
