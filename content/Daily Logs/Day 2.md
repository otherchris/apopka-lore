16 Kythorn, 1325 DR

The adventurers awaken at the Entwhistle campsite. (8 am)

- Note there is a potion shop and stables in this village
- [[Coristine Sloven]] and his driver [[Osgood]] are leaving for [[The City of Millspar]] and say their goodbyes
- Visit the [[The Potion Shop]], right now Potions of Healing are the only item for sale, 50gp
- Potion shop proprietor assures party the shop is not a "front"
- [[Getonda Goodfoot]] earns 2gp buffooning for the villagers
- Revisit the bulletin board in [[The Quail's Egg]]. (9 am)
- [[Getonda Goodfoot]] orders breakfast
- The party visits [[Farmer Pickle]]
- They tell [[Farmer Pickle]] they will "check out" the disappearing cows
- The party explores [[Locations/Entwhistle/The Cave Under Farmer Pickle's Field|The Cave Under Farmer Pickle's Field]]
- They find a very old skeleton
- They find a bloody scene of eaten cows and dismembered adventurers
- They battle a huge insect-like monster (11 am)
- Meet [[Dakota]] and [[Rickward]]. Their friends were killed by the bug monster and they have been trapped in the cave without a light. The party shared light and food with them and were subsequently robbed.
- In the cave they find a pit. 20 ft across, and despite many creative attempts, could not see the bottom. There is climbing gear nearby, and there are _barely_ traversable footholds going down as far as could be seen. A skeleton nearby was found with the [[Journal of the Skeleton by the Pit in the Field Cave]]
- Leaving the cave, they return to [[Farmer Pickle]]'s house. There, [[Dakota]] and [[Rickward]] are found to have claimed the reward and stolen the money and smokes. The party chases them down and retrieves their goods. At [[Dakota]]'s behest, [[Rickward]] tells the party that "magical treasure" can be found in the ruins north of the village. (3 pm)
- The party returns to the Quail's Egg for some food and to revisit the bulletin board. [[Crognar Crumpknot]] invites the party to dinner "tonight or maybe tomorrow night" at [[Fildara]]'s house. 
- The party goes to [[Wizard's Tower Brewing Co.]] to take up the "basement related work". They meet [[Geezo]] the proprietor and go into the basement to make it safe for workers. After battling giant zombie rats, giant centipedes and a horrifying worm monster they find two healing potions, a wizards book containing the spell False Life, and a small purple-black orb.
